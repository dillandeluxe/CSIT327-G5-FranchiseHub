Introduction

Welcome to FranchiseHub. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform. By accessing or using FranchiseHub, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

We are dedicated to maintaining the trust and confidence of our users. This policy is designed to help you understand what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our services:

Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Username
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Profile picture (optional)

Account Details

  • User role (Franchisee/Franchisor)
  • Security questions and encrypted answers
  • Account creation date
  • Login history

Usage Data

  • Pages visited
  • Franchise applications submitted
  • Search queries
  • Device and browser information
  • IP address

Application Data

  • Franchise application details
  • Business experience information
  • Communication preferences
  • Status updates

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various purposes:

01

Account Management

To create and maintain your account, verify your identity, and enable account recovery through security questions.

02

Platform Operations

To connect franchisees with franchisors, process applications, and facilitate communication between parties.

03

Service Improvement

To analyze usage patterns, improve features, fix bugs, and enhance overall user experience.

04

Security & Fraud Prevention

To detect and prevent unauthorized access, fraudulent activities, and ensure platform integrity.

05

Communication

To send important updates, security notifications, and respond to your inquiries and support requests.

Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

Encryption: All sensitive data, including passwords and security answers, are encrypted using industry-standard algorithms.

Secure Hosting: Our platform is hosted on Render with Supabase database, both providing enterprise-level security.

Access Controls: Strict role-based access ensures users can only view and modify data they're authorized to access.

Regular Monitoring: We continuously monitor for suspicious activities and potential security threats.

Information Sharing Policy

We do not sell your personal information. Your data is shared only in the following limited circumstances:

When Information May Be Shared:

  • Between Users: When you submit a franchise application, relevant information is shared with the franchisor to facilitate the connection.
  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party services (hosting, database) that help us operate the platform under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government request.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the acquiring entity.

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience on FranchiseHub:

Essential Cookies

Required for the platform to function properly. These include session management, authentication, and security features.

Functional Cookies

Remember your preferences and settings to provide a personalized experience.

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how users interact with the platform to improve features and performance.

You have control over cookies: You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect platform functionality. Most browsers allow you to refuse all cookies or indicate when a cookie is being sent.
